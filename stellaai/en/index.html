FAQ

Q: How do I restore my membership?

A: Sign in with the Stella AI account you used when purchasing, then tap “Restore purchases” on the membership screen. An Apple subscription can usually be linked to only one Stella AI account.

Q: How do I cancel auto-renewal?

A: Go to iPhone Settings → your name (Apple Account) → Subscriptions, select Stella AI and cancel. Your membership stays active until the end of the current billing period.

Q: How do I unlock the second character?

A: Subscribe to Deep Love · Monthly or the Annual plan, then unlock and switch on the “Change character” screen.

Q: How do I delete my account?

A: In the app, go to Me → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and related data. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it in Settings first.

Q: How can I get help?

A: Email 869922457@qq.com or send feedback in the app. We handle requests as quickly as possible on business days (China Standard Time, UTC+8).
