Stella AI (“App” or “we”) is an AI virtual-character emotional companionship and chat application. We follow applicable laws including the Cybersecurity Law, Personal Information Protection Law, and Data Security Law of the People’s Republic of China in preparing this Privacy Policy.

Please read this Policy before use, especially how chat is sent to third-party AI models, location for weather, camera/photo permissions, account deletion, and password reset. If you disagree, stop using the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only personal information necessary for App functions:

1.1 Account information

  • Email address: registration, sign-in, password recovery (required)
  • Password: stored encrypted by the auth service; we cannot see plaintext
  • User ID: system-generated identifier to link your data
  • Avatar: optional image from camera or photo library

1.2 Chat and companionship content

  • Text you send to AI characters and AI replies
  • Character settings: name, how they address you, personality tags
  • Mood journal entries (daily score and notes)
  • Memory items, special moments, anniversaries and birthdays you provide
  • Affection level, relationship stage, chapter unlock progress

Do not enter ID numbers, bank cards, passwords, verification codes, keys, or other sensitive secrets in chat.

1.3 Location (optional, weather only)

  • With your permission for daily greeting weather, we read latitude/longitude via system location
  • Coordinates query third-party weather and reverse-geocoding for city weather and forecast
  • We do not upload or store your coordinates on Stella AI servers; results are cached locally on device (~1 hour)
  • You may deny location; only weather display is affected

1.4 Camera and photos (optional)

  • Camera/photos are used only when you upload an avatar
  • We read only the single image you select; we do not scan or upload other photos

1.5 Subscriptions and transactions

  • Apple IAP receipts and original transaction ID
  • Product ID, subscription status, expiry, auto-renew status
  • We do not collect Apple ID passwords, card numbers, or payment passwords; Apple handles payment

1.6 Device and logs

  • Device model, OS version, app version, language and timezone (UI and local notifications)
  • Network status and IP address (logged when processing requests)
  • Crash/error logs and failed API records for stability

1.7 What we do not collect

  • Contacts, SMS, call logs
  • Microphone/audio (no voice feature currently)
  • ID documents, face, fingerprint, or other biometrics
  • No third-party ad SDKs; no IDFA for ads
  • No third-party analytics SDKs for ad profiling/tracking

2. How We Use Information

2.1 Core service

  • Register, sign in, reset password, delete account
  • Generate AI replies, chapter stories, daily greeting text
  • Show affection, relationship, memory album, mood journal
  • Weather summary where authorized
  • Verify membership, free allowance, subscription validity

2.2 Reminders

With notification permission, we schedule local notifications at times you set (default 8:00, 13:00, 20:00) for character greetings. The App does not use remote push; content is generated and scheduled on device. You may disable in App or system settings.

2.3 Security and compliance

  • Detect abnormal login, quota abuse, cracking, misuse
  • Secure service and transactions; handle complaints and disputes
  • Comply with lawful regulatory requests

2.4 Improvement

We may use de-identified, aggregated statistics to improve the product. We do not use your chat text to train our models; if we ever need to, we will ask for explicit consent and offer an easy opt-out.

3. Storage and Security

3.1 Where data lives

  • On device: chat history, character settings, mood, memories, relationship/chapter progress, preferences, weather cache, entitlement cache—in local DB and preferences
  • Cloud: account (email, hashed password, user ID), avatar files, subscription records only
  • Servers and storage are in mainland China; cross-border transfer would follow separate legal notice and consent

3.2 Chat storage (important)

Chat with AI characters is stored locally on your device by default; our servers do not store chat body text. To generate replies, your messages, needed context, and character settings are sent in real time to third-party AI (see Section 4). Uninstalling, clearing data, or changing devices may make local chat unrecoverable.

3.3 Retention

  • Account: until you delete the account
  • Avatar: until replaced or account deleted
  • Subscriptions: while active; transaction records may be kept as required by law
  • Local data: until you delete in App, delete account, or uninstall
  • Logs: generally up to 6 months unless law requires longer

3.4 Security measures

  • Transit: HTTPS/TLS for all network requests
  • Storage: passwords hashed by auth service; row-level access control on server data
  • Access: minimal internal access to user data
  • Keys: AI API keys kept on server, not in the client

Internet security is not absolute. If a personal data incident occurs, we will notify you and regulators as required by law.

3.5 Password reset notice

“Forgot password” lets you set a new password with your registered email; no old password and no email verification code in the current version. This aids recovery but means anyone who knows your email could reset your password. Protect your email; avoid shared emails. We plan to add email verification in a future version.

4. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell personal information. We share or delegate processing only when necessary:

• AI model provider (DeepSeek)

  • Shared: your chat text, necessary context, character persona prompts
  • Purpose: AI replies, chapter stories, greeting text
  • Method: via our server API (api.hujiaofenwritingcat.top), encrypted to the model; we do not persist chat body on our server
  • Not shared: email, avatar, location, contacts, payment info
  • Provider processing is subject to its own privacy policy

• Cloud provider (Supabase)

  • Shared: email, hashed password, user ID, avatar, subscription records
  • Purpose: authentication, file storage, subscription sync

• Apple

  • Shared: IAP/subscription requests, receipts, restore requests
  • Purpose: payment, subscription management, entitlement verification; Apple’s privacy policy applies

• Weather and geolocation (Open-Meteo, BigDataCloud)

  • Shared: latitude/longitude when you authorize
  • Purpose: current weather, forecast, city name
  • Only when weather is enabled; no account identifiers attached

Legal disclosure

We may disclose information when required by law, valid government/judicial requests, to protect rights/safety/property, or in emergencies.

Business transfers

If we merge, are acquired, or transfer assets, we will require the successor to honor this Policy or obtain your consent again.

5. Your Rights

5.1 Access and copy

View account, subscription, chat, mood, and memories in the App. For copies in other formats, email support.

5.2 Correct and update

Edit avatar, character name, address terms, tags under Me; edit or delete mood entries in App.

5.3 Delete

  • Chat: clear local chat in Settings
  • Memories: hide or delete items in the memory album
  • Account: Settings → Delete account removes server account/avatar and local data for that account
  • Uninstall: removes all local data

Account deletion is irreversible.

5.4 Withdraw consent

  • Location: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Stella AI
  • Camera/Photos: revoke in system settings
  • Notifications: disable in App or system settings

Withdrawal affects only the related feature; prior processing based on consent remains lawful.

5.5 Requests

Email 869922457@qq.com to exercise rights. We may verify identity (e.g. from registered email). We respond within 15 business days where possible; if we refuse, we explain why and how to appeal.

6. Minors

This App is adult emotional companionship; we do not offer virtual partners or virtual intimate relationships to minors. We do not knowingly collect minors’ personal information.

  • Under 18: do not register or use the App.
  • If we collected a minor’s data without guardian consent, we will delete it and close the account promptly.
  • Guardians who find minor use or payments may contact support; we will verify and assist, including appeal channels.
