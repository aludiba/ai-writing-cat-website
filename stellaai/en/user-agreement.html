1. Service Description

1.1 About the App

Stella AI is a virtual-character emotional companionship and chat application powered by artificial intelligence. Core features include:

  • AI chat companionship: Text conversations with virtual AI characters that reply according to their configured personality and tone
  • Character setup: Choose built-in characters (tsundere “Yu Han”, gentle “Xiao Yu”) and customize name, how they address you, and personality tags
  • Affection and relationship: Affection level (Lv.1–100) and relationship stages (First meeting, Familiar, Ambiguous, Confession, Lovers, Soulmate) based on interaction
  • Chapter stories: AI-driven story chapters per character, unlocked as affection increases
  • Memory album: Curated chat highlights and special moments; you may hide or delete entries
  • Mood journal: Daily mood scores and notes
  • Daily greetings and reminders: Local notifications at times you set for morning, afternoon, and evening greetings from AI characters, which may include local weather summaries
  • Stickers and input aids: Built-in emoji/sticker assets and typing suggestions

We may add, adjust, or remove features as the product evolves; the version you use controls what is available.

1.2 Nature of AI Characters (Important)

  • All characters are virtual AI characters, not real natural persons. They have no real identity, real emotions, legal personality, or capacity to act in the real world.
  • All dialogue, stories, updates, and “memories” are generated by AI models from character settings and product rules.
  • AI characters assume no real-world obligations or promises because of your payment, interaction, or companionship.
  • Companionship and emotional support do not constitute and cannot replace real human relationships, psychological counseling, medical diagnosis, legal advice, investment advice, or other professional services. If you are in serious emotional distress or crisis, contact qualified professionals or your local crisis helpline promptly.

1.3 Accounts

The App uses email + password accounts. Register with a valid email; passwords must be at least 8 characters and include both letters and numbers. Keep your credentials secure; you are responsible for consequences of disclosure or unauthorized use due to your own actions.

If you forget your password, reset it in the App via “Forgot password” using your registered email. Important: for easier recovery, reset does not require your old password. Keep your registration email confidential; use an email only you control when possible.

You may delete your account anytime under Settings → Delete account. Deletion permanently removes server-side account data and avatar, and clears on-device data linked to that account (chats, characters, relationships, memories, etc.). Deletion is irreversible; purchased subscriptions are not refunded because of account deletion.

3. Subscriptions and Paid Services

3.1 Free allowance

Non-subscribers may use a limited number of AI character replies (currently 50 character-generated replies; your own messages are not counted). After the allowance is used, a subscription is required to continue AI chat. Allowance amounts may change by version; in-app display controls.

3.2 Plans

On iOS, the App offers auto-renewing subscriptions via Apple App Store In-App Purchase:

  • Light monthly: unlimited chat, unlock second character (price as shown in the App and App Store)
  • Deep monthly: full features including second character, faster affection, exclusive address terms, priority response, etc. (price as shown in the App and App Store)
  • Yearly: all Deep benefits for one year (price as shown in the App and App Store)

Actual price, billing period, free-trial eligibility, and benefits are as shown in the App and on the App Store. Eligible plans may include a free trial per Apple rules.

3.3 Auto-renewal

  • Subscription fees are charged to your Apple ID when you confirm purchase.
  • Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, the subscription renews automatically for the selected period and you are charged again.
  • Renewal charges occur within 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Manage or cancel under iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. After canceling, benefits continue until the end of the paid period.
  • Canceling during a free trial ends the trial immediately; typically one free trial per user per subscription group.

3.4 Subscription and account binding

Purchases via Apple IAP bind on our servers to your currently signed-in Stella AI account. One Apple subscription can bind to only one Stella AI account. If you sign in with another account and try to restore, you may be told the subscription is bound elsewhere—sign in with the account used at purchase to keep benefits.

3.5 Restore purchases

On the subscription screen, tap “Restore purchases” to restore valid subscriptions tied to your Apple ID, subject to binding rules above.

3.6 Refunds

Under Apple policy, App Store subscription charges are generally non-refundable except where law requires otherwise. Request refunds through Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com); we cannot process App Store refunds directly.

3.7 Platform scope

Subscriptions and IAP are currently available on iOS only. Other platforms will be announced when paid features launch there.
