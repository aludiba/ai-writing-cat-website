Welcome to Stella AI auto-renewal membership!

Before subscribing, read and understand this Agreement, especially auto-renewal, billing timing, cancellation, and refund rules. Confirming a purchase means you agree. If you do not agree, do not subscribe.

1. Service Description

1.1 Auto-renewal service

Auto-renewal means: after you buy an auto-renewing subscription through the Apple App Store, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends, the system renews and charges you automatically so membership continues without interruption.

1.2 Plans

The App currently offers these auto-renewing subscriptions (see App Store for details):

  • Light monthly: 1-month period
  • Deep monthly: 1-month period
  • Yearly: 1-year period (all Deep benefits)

Price, benefits, and promotions are as shown in the App Store and in the App at purchase.

1.3 Renewal rules

  • Renewal period matches the plan you chose
  • Renewal price is the price shown on the App Store at renewal
  • If prices change, Apple’s rules apply for notice; you may cancel before the change takes effect

2. Billing

2.1 Timing

  • Initial purchase is charged to your Apple ID immediately upon confirmation
  • Auto-renewal charges within 24 hours before the current period ends
  • Exact charge time is determined by Apple; we cannot control the precise moment

2.2 Payment method

  • Charges use the payment method linked to your Apple ID and are processed by Apple
  • We cannot access your card, payment passwords, or other payment credentials
  • If renewal payment fails, membership ends when the current paid period expires

2.3 Successful renewal

  • After a successful charge, membership extends by one period
  • You keep the benefits of your plan tier

3. Cancel renewal

3.1 How to cancel

You may cancel auto-renewal anytime, for example:

  • iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Stella AI → Cancel Subscription
  • Or via App Store account subscription management

3.2 Rules after canceling

  • Benefits for the current paid period remain until expiry
  • After expiry, no further charges; membership ends
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before period end; check the renewal date in subscription settings

4. Subscription and account binding

  • Purchase is via Apple ID and binds on our servers to your currently signed-in Stella AI account
  • One Apple subscription typically binds to only one Stella AI account
  • Use “Restore purchases” in the App to restore valid subscriptions via Apple ID, subject to binding rules

5. Refunds

  • Under Apple policy, App Store subscription charges are generally non-refundable except where law requires
  • Request refunds via Apple (e.g. reportaproblem.apple.com); Apple reviews requests
  • We cannot process App Store refunds directly

6. Changes and interruptions

  • We may adjust subscription benefits as the product evolves; material changes will be communicated in the App or other applicable channels
  • Outages from network, maintenance, Apple service issues, or force majeure: we will use reasonable efforts to restore service but do not guarantee zero interruption
